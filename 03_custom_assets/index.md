# Chapter 3: Custom Tile & Object Creator

One of Lidarion Hexmap Studio's most powerful capabilities is its integrated **Asset Workshop**. You can take any PNG, JPG, or WebP graphic—whether generated by AI, downloaded from the web, or hand-drawn—and convert it into a standard, gapless hextile or map prop without ever leaving Foundry VTT.

## In this Chapter

* **[The Custom Tile Creator Workshop](tile_creator_guide.html)**
  * Launching the Creator for Custom Terrain vs. Custom Objects.
  * Understanding the 3 Template Zones: Core Hex (Green), Overhang Zone (Blue), and Cutoff Zone (Red).
  * The "Mask blue overhang zone" toggle for flat tiles.
  * Canvas controls: Panning, mouse-wheel zoom, full-width zoom slider (1%–500%), decimal input, and 1px keyboard arrow nudges.
  * Multi-Variant Tabs: Creating complete 4-variant tile families in one go.
  * Managing custom categories, subcategories, and dynamic accent colors.
  * Saving, organizing, and safe right-click deletion with permanent disk cleanup.
