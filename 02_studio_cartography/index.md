# Chapter 2: Hexmap Studio & Map Building

Hexmap Studio transforms your Foundry canvas into an interactive cartography workstation. This chapter covers the interface, the four drawing layers, drawing tools, palette management, and path network creation.

---

## In this Chapter

* **[Tools, Layers & Painting](tools_and_painting.html)**
  * Launching Hexmap Studio and navigating the workspace.
  * The 4 Layers: Terrain, Custom Terrain, Objects, and Roads & Rivers.
  * Universal drawing tools: Brush, Flood Fill, Eraser, and Eyedropper.
  * Brush Size slider (1–6 hex radius) and live glowing hover previews.
  * Random Variant shuffling vs. specific variant selection.
  * The 1-Terrain + 1-Object stacking rule.
  * Canvas navigation, Infinite expansion, and Undo/Redo history.
* **[Roads & Rivers Network](roads_and_rivers.html)**
  * Drawing vector road connections with natural dotted trail aesthetics.
  * Drawing winding rivers with soft translucent bank blending.
  * Step-based line width presets and color selectors.
  * Editing, extending, and erasing path segments.
