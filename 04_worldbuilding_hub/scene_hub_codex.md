# The Interactive Scene Hub (Codex)

The **Scene Hub** is one of Lidarion's crown jewels: a gorgeous, full-featured campaign atlas and regional codex that turns static hex coordinates into living lore entries, encounter tables, and battlemap transitions.

---

## 1. Accessing the Scene Hub

You can open the Scene Hub in multiple convenient ways:
* **Canvas Click**: Click on any territory or location on your map when using the selection tool.
* **Left Toolbar Tool**: Click **"Lidarion - Tools"** (<i class="fa-regular fa-circle-l"></i>) → **"Scene Hub"** (<i class="fa-solid fa-map"></i>) to open the hub for the active scene.
* **Dashboard Button**: Click the Hub icon next to any territory in the *Hex Regions & Locations* dashboard.

---

## 2. Interactive Artwork Banner

Every region and location can be personalized with a wide, cinematic artwork banner:

* **Upload Artwork**: Click the banner image button to select an illustration, landscape concept art, or coat of arms.
* **Interactive Pan & Zoom**:
  * Click **"Adjust Image"**: Drag your mouse to position the illustration vertically or horizontally.
  * Scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in or out until the composition is framed perfectly.
  * Click **"Lock Image"** to freeze the framing.
* **Full-Screen View**: Click the expand icon to view the banner art in high-resolution full size.

---

## 3. Notes & Lore Editor

The center of the Hub is a rich-text workspace for world-building:

* **Rich Formatting**: Full support for markdown, headings (H1, H2, H3), bulleted lists, blockquotes, and tables.
* **Auto-Save Toggle**: Edits are automatically saved in real-time as you write (`Auto-saved` indicator).
* **Dynamic Table of Contents**:
  * On the left side of the hub, an expandable **Table of Contents (TOC)** automatically parses your headings (H1, H2, H3) and creates clickable jump-links for instant navigation through long articles.

---

## 4. Linked Scene (Overland-to-Battlemap Transitions)

You can link any tactical battlemap, city map, or dungeon level directly to a location on your hexmap:

1. Locate the target scene in Foundry's standard **Scenes** directory.
2. Drag the scene card and drop it onto the **"Drop scene here to link"** slot in the Hub.
3. Once linked:
   * **Left-Click "View Map"**: Immediately transitions your GM canvas to that battlemap.
   * **Right-Click Menu**: Allows you to **Activate Scene** directly for players or open the Scene Configuration sheet.

---

## 5. Linked Documents Binder

Bring all your campaign prep together by associating relevant Foundry documents with each territory:

### Supported Document Types
* **<i class="fa-solid fa-user"></i> Actors**: Regional rulers, quest-givers, wandering monsters, bandit warbands, and dungeon bosses.
* **<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i> Items**: Quest artifacts, magical loot, regional trade commodities, or special herbs found in this biome.
* **<i class="fa-solid fa-book-open"></i> Journals**: Lore handouts, historical texts, rumors, and investigation clues.
* **<i class="fa-solid fa-dice-d20"></i> Roll Tables**: Regional random encounter tables, weather rolls, or foraging tables.
* **<i class="fa-solid fa-cards"></i> Card Stacks**: Mystery cards, fate decks, or hazard cards.

### How to Link Documents
* Simply drag any Actor, Item, Journal, or RollTable from Foundry's sidebars and drop it into the Hub's document dropzone.
* Documents are automatically organized into clean, collapsible categories with count badges.
* **Left-Click**: Instantly opens the document sheet.
* **Right-Click**: Allows you to unlink the document or configure player ownership permissions.

---

## 6. Navigation & Safe Archiving

* **Favorites Bookmarking**: Click the star icon (<i class="fa-solid fa-star"></i>) at the top of any Hub entry to bookmark it in your favorites drawer.
* **Browser-Style History Navigation**: Use the **Back** (<i class="fa-solid fa-arrow-left"></i>) and **Forward** (<i class="fa-solid fa-arrow-right"></i>) buttons to step through territories you have recently viewed.
* **Protected Journal Storage**:
  * All hub notes, banner settings, and linked documents are automatically mirrored to a dedicated Foundry Journal Entry: `{Scene Name} (Hexmap Hub)`.
  * This guarantees that your world-building data is safe, backed up, and seamlessly exportable with your Foundry world.
