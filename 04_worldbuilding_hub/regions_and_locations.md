# Regions & Locations

Lidarion makes it easy to partition your map into political realms, dangerous wilderness biomes, and notable points of interest.

---

## 1. Entering "Create Regions" Mode

In the top header of Hexmap Studio, switch the mode toggle from **Build Hexmap** to **Create Regions** (<i class="fa-solid fa-map-location-dot"></i>). The palette updates to display your territories and points of interest.

Beneath the header, you can switch between two sub-modes:
* **Regions**: Large territorial areas (kingdoms, baronies, biomes, magical zones).
* **Locations**: Specific landmarks and points of interest (cities, ruins, dungeons, watchtowers).

---

## 2. Painting Regions (Territories & Biomes)

Regions are represented as translucent, color-coded overlays with crisp border contours:

1. **Create a Region**:
   * Click **"Create New Region"** in the palette.
   * Choose a descriptive **Region Name** (e.g., *Kingdom of Valdoria*, *Whispering Woods*, *Obsidian Wastes*).
   * Pick a distinctive **Color** for the region's territorial wash.
2. **Paint Hexes into the Region**:
   * Select the region in your list.
   * Use the **Brush Tool** (with Brush Size 1–6) to click or drag across the map.
   * Every painted hex is immediately assigned to this territory, displaying the region's color tint.
3. **Removing Hexes**:
   * Select the **Eraser Tool** and click or drag over hexes to unassign them from the territory.
4. **Interactive Hover Tooltips**:
   * When exploring or viewing the map, pausing your cursor over any painted hex displays a clean tooltip showing the territory's name.

---

## 3. Creating Locations (Points of Interest)

Locations represent focal points of adventure:

1. **Create a Location**:
   * Switch to the **Locations** sub-tab and click **"Create New Location"**.
   * Enter the name of your landmark (e.g. *Sunken Citadel*, *Port Silver*, *Dragon's Rest*).
   * Choose a thematic color.
2. **Assigning the Hex Footprint**:
   * Paint the specific hex or cluster of hexes where the location resides.
3. **Discovery Notifications**:
   * When players move tokens into a location for the first time, Lidarion can trigger a dramatic on-screen banner: `NEW LOCATION DISCOVERED`.

---

## 4. The Hex Regions & Locations Dashboard

Outside of Hexmap Studio, Gamemasters can manage all regions and locations from a dedicated control dashboard:

1. In Foundry's left canvas toolbar, click the **"Lidarion - Tools"** control icon (<i class="fa-regular fa-circle-l"></i>).
2. Click the **"Edit Hex Regions & Locations"** button (<i class="fa-solid fa-hexagon-nodes"></i>).
3. The dashboard provides:
   * A searchable list of all regions and locations on the current map.
   * Real-time hex tile counts for each territory.
   * Quick-jump buttons to open the interactive **Scene Hub** for each entry.
   * One-click deletion with safety confirmation prompts.
